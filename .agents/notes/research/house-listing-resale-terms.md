# House-listing resale terms

> Date / fetched: 2026-10-10 · Type: research, not a product decision or launch approval
> Scope: [#313](https://github.com/zevium-dev/core/issues/313) · [House-supply decision](../decisions/2026-10-10-house-supply-via-aggregators.md)

**treg permits the hosted platform/billing model. No specific house listing is cleared end to end by this review.** RapidAPI and Brave prohibit the proposed resale under their standard terms; the other direct vendors require permission or leave the marketplace case unresolved. The requested 3–5 clearly allowed listings cannot be substantiated from these sources. Four permission candidates appear below; none is a substitute for an allowed verdict.

I am not a lawyer; this is a contract-screening record for legal and vendor review.

## Scope and method

The proposed transaction is: Zevium owns and pays for upstream credentials, accepts arbitrary consumer/agent requests through its gateway, returns upstream responses, and charges consumers per call. Ordinary commercial app integration, affiliate referrals, ownership of output, and permission to run agents do not individually establish permission for that transaction.

Verdicts are research interpretations of this model under public terms, without assuming a negotiated agreement. In particular, treating an interchangeable lookup as a similar/competitive service is an inference, not a vendor ruling:

- **Allowed**: affirmative permission, limited to the stated layer and conditions.
- **Forbidden**: standard terms expressly prohibit the proposed activity; a negotiated exception could change this.
- **Needs written permission**: terms provide a consent/contract route, or a relevant partner agreement must be obtained before this use.
- **Unclear**: available grants and restrictions do not resolve the proposed marketplace use. Keep the launch gate closed pending clarification.

## Aggregator findings

### treg

[Terms §§4, 5, 7, 8, 11](https://treg.to/terms), fetched 2026-10-10: §11 expressly contemplates downstream customer billing with `X-Treg-Meta` customer tags and invoices from `usage/by-tag`; §7 distinguishes this hosted use from redistributing the software. §8 expressly covers metered calls using treg's provider keys. Agent calls are anticipated in §4, with responsibility assigned to the token owner. §7 prohibits upstream-terms violations and disguising automated traffic.

**Scope limit:** the platform permission does not disclose treg's contracts with each vendor or establish that they authorize onward distribution of every response. No blanket sublicensing or response-cache grant was found. Confirm the named upstream, paid plan, operation, and onward rights before promoting a listing to allowed. Pass-through customer attribution here means usage tagging; it is not a general waiver of vendor branding requirements. Software reuse remains a separate [license blocker](../findings/treg-reuse.md).

### RapidAPI / Nokia

[Terms, Service / API Rights / Restrictions / Payments](https://rapidapi.com/page/terms), fetched 2026-10-10: Nokia of America Corporation dba Rapid grants a limited, non-sublicensable marketplace license. Restrictions also prohibit building a competing service. Zevium's proposed marketplace is a direct concern under that restriction, independently of API-provider consent.

API-use rights are separately contracted between each provider and consumer. Rapid facilitates billing and expressly does not act as the provider's reseller. A subscription therefore supplies neither blanket API/response redistribution rights nor a uniform cache license. Record each listing's API Content/Terms and any custom agreement. Approval from Rapid alone cannot resolve vendor restrictions; vendor approval alone cannot resolve Rapid's marketplace restriction. Seek an express marketplace exception from Rapid and onward API/output rights from each provider.

## First listings to clear

**Clearly allowed end-to-end shortlist: none established.** The allowed treg platform layer is useful, but it cannot fill a 3–5-listing launch checklist by itself. Recommended first four candidates, in permission-work order (research recommendation, not a change to the accepted sourcing order):

| Candidate house listing | Why pursue it first                                            | Evidence required before launch                                                                            |
| ----------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Exa web search          | Explicit API-reseller partner track.                           | Signed Exa permission for this operation, directly or an applicable treg onward-rights confirmation.       |
| Tavily web search       | Explicit Marketplace partner track matches tool catalogs.      | Authorized partner terms covering Zevium consumers, output and billing, or documented aggregator coverage. |
| Firecrawl page scrape   | Gateway program provides organization-funded downstream usage. | Reviewed/accepted agreement permitting the exact charging model and approved account topology.             |
| SerpApi SERP lookup     | Terms specify a written-permission route.                      | Provider's express §2 permission; if through treg, documented rights for this exact downstream use.        |

For each candidate, preserve the vendor's written response/agreement reference, legal entity, plan, named operations, allowed end-user and agent uses, raw/derived output rights, cache duration/deletion requirements, attribution, geography, and expiry. Re-fetch terms before launch. Private contracts belong in restricted storage; commit only the approval reference and scope.

Suggested permission request (not sent):

> Zevium operates a prepaid, per-call API marketplace. We hold and pay for upstream accounts, keep keys server-side, and route requests from unrelated human and AI-agent customers. Customers receive API responses and pay Zevium; they do not buy your individual subscriptions. Please confirm in writing whether this model is permitted for the named APIs, including raw response redistribution, downstream automated use, caching, attribution and branding. If it requires a reseller/partner agreement, please provide it. For aggregator supply, please confirm that the applicable agreement authorizes this onward distribution to Zevium's customers.

Legal risk and business economics are separate: negotiated margins, monthly minimums, uptime commitments and subsidies still need review after rights are cleared. A cheap upstream or a working endpoint cannot satisfy #313's resale gate.
